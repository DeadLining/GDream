# AI Comic Studio v0.7.0 Release Audit

Release branch: `feat/v0.7-full-episode-loop`

## Feature Gates

- One persistent 12-stage `EpisodeAIRun` owns the existing-structure-to-publication lifecycle.
- Approved character references and panels are reused after interruption; failed stages can retry without regenerating immutable assets.
- Character dialogue and narration both synthesize through the configured Qwen TTS channel.
- Every approved shot generates and activates a Wan image-to-video asset.
- Remotion stages panels, shot videos, speech, and sound assets inside its public job directory.
- Rendering reuses an installed Chrome executable when available and allows large media to initialize before timeout.
- The final render must pass automated review, release gate approval, and internal publication persistence.

## Real Model Evidence

- MiniMax-M3 generated and structured one episode with 2 characters, 3 scenes, 5 shots, and 3 spoken/narrated lines.
- GPT image generation produced 2 character references and 5 shot panels.
- Qwen3-TTS produced 3 WAV assets, including a narration line without a character binding.

- Internal publication status: `published`.
- Browser E2E: the selected shot MP4 and final review MP4 both exposed playable video metadata; `1 passed`.
